Developer Modding

Unlimited Asset Customization

IconCreator is built to be an open platform for your creativity. You aren't limited to built-in assets—you can easily expand the application with your own custom fonts, backgrounds, frames, and stickers.

1

Custom Typography (.TTF / .OTF)

Drop your favorite .ttf or .otf font files into the Android project directory app/src/main/assets/Fonts/. The app automatically scans and lists them in the font dropdown.

2

Unique Background Textures

Add custom textures, bricks, or artwork as .png files into app/src/main/assets/Backgrounds/. Perfect for custom theme branding.

3

Custom Frame Borders

Create your own transparent PNG frames and place them in app/src/main/assets/Borders/. These will automatically populate in the Border frame picker.

4

Personal Overlay Stickers

Drop logos, badges, or stickers into app/src/main/assets/Decor/. Use the in-app position controls to overlay your signature badge.
