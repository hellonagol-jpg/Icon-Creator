Unlimited Customization

IconCreator is built to be a platform for your creativity. You aren't limited to the assets we provide—you can easily expand the program's library with your own design elements.

1

Custom Typography

Drop your favorite .ttf or .otf font files into the app/src/main/assets/Fonts/ directory. The app will automatically detect and list them in the text settings.

2

Unique Backgrounds

Add your own textures, patterns, or artwork as .png files to app/src/main/assets/Backgrounds/. Perfect for branding or specific themes.

3

Custom Framing

Create your own transparent .png frames and place them in app/src/main/assets/Borders/. These will appear in the Border selection menu.

4

Personal Decorations

Drop logos, badges, or stickers into app/src/main/assets/Decor/. Use the in-app controls to position them as your signature layer.
