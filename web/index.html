Unlimited Customization

IconCreator is built to be expanded. You can easily add your own assets to the program by dropping them into the assets folder before building.

1

Custom Fonts

Drop .ttf or .otf files into assets/Fonts/ to use them for your icon text.

2

Custom Backgrounds

Add .png textures or gradients to assets/Backgrounds/ to expand your library.

3

Custom Borders

Place transparent .png frames in assets/Borders/ to create unique framing styles.

4

Custom Decorations

Drop badges, stickers, or logos into assets/Decor/ for the final decoration layer.
